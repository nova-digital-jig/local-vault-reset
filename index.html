The problem

Setup keeps moving. Your decisions do not.

You add folders, dashboards, and agents, but last week's decision is still hard to find. Another vault tour will not fix an unclear source of truth.

This course works on a deliberately small lab: one folder, three questions, six artifacts, and a test that can actually fail.
