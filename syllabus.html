Local Vault Reset syllabus

One safe folder. Six artifacts. Two tests.

Complete the lessons in order. Each lesson produces evidence; understanding the idea without creating the artifact does not count as completion.

Module 1 · 30 minutes

Bound the reset

  1. Define done: choose one safe folder and three retrieval questions.
  2. Close restricted data: write a denylist and owner-only gates.

Module 2 · 40 minutes

See the system

  1. Inventory without reorganizing: map roles, sources, conflicts, and readers.
  2. Keep, kill, sequence: choose what stays active and order three changes.

Module 3 · 40 minutes

Build one retrieval path

  1. Create the minimum index: route questions to authoritative sources.
  2. Test retrieval: answer three known questions within ten minutes total.

Module 4 · 45 minutes

Govern changes

  1. Classify actions: separate local, review, and owner-only work.
  2. Write, verify, roll back: execute one narrow change with visible proof.

Module 5 · 45 minutes

Keep it alive

  1. Run the weekly reset: advance work instead of passively checking health.
  2. Capstone: prove retrieval and one governed change.

You need

A local folder and a text editor.

  • Adult learner or authorized business representative
  • Three hours of focused work
  • A backup or untouched lab copy
  • Non-sensitive or synthetic example notes
  • No paid plugin, LMS, or model download

Completion

You pass by showing the path works.

All six artifacts must be non-empty. Three questions must reach their source notes within ten minutes total. One reversible change must show its before state, classification, visible verification, and rollback. The rubric requires 12/14, with no zero in privacy, retrieval, or governance.

Not included

No private vault access or done-for-you rebuild.

The course does not include migration, plugin or model installation, account access, restricted-data review, legal/security advice, ongoing coaching, or guaranteed results. The guided $197 Local Vault Diagnostic is a separate controlled offer.
