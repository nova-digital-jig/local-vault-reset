Five checks

A useful vault should answer known questions safely.

01

Define three questions

Write three decisions or facts you should be able to retrieve without browsing the whole vault.

02

Name the authority

For each question, identify the note or record that wins when two sources disagree.

03

Time retrieval

Reach all three source notes within ten minutes total. Record misses instead of changing the test.

04

Close restricted data

Keep journals, client files, credentials, unpublished research, interview records, and thesis material outside the lab.

05

Prove one rollback

Make one narrow change only after capturing its before state, checkpoint, visible result, and rollback.
